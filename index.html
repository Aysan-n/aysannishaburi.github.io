About me

Aysan Nishaburi

I am a PhD student at the Chair for Cryptography at Ruhr University Bochum, supervised by Eike Kiltz.

My research focuses on the foundations of cryptography and provable security. I am particularly interested in password-authenticated key exchange (PAKE), multi-instance problems, and the security guarantees of cryptographic protocols.

Research interests

  • Provable and concrete security
  • Password-authenticated key exchange (PAKE)
  • Multi-instance cryptographic problems
